A change in the kelp canopy can mean something quite different to a diver, a satellite observer and a coastal resident. KnowOcean brings those scales together to ask what we can learn about the ocean and where our view is still incomplete.

  • Our mission — the questions that guide the journal.
  • Editorial approach — how we read evidence and handle uncertainty.
  • Author profiles — the people behind the bylines.
  • Partnerships — disclosures and collaboration inquiries.
