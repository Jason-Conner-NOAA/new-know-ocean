Start with the observation

What was measured, where, and over what period? We aim to identify the survey, dataset or paper behind a claim so readers can trace it back to its source.

Show what the evidence can support

A warmer reading at one buoy is not a trend across a whole bay. We distinguish measurements from model estimates and explain when more than one interpretation fits the evidence.

Leave room for correction

If a figure, attribution or explanation needs another look, see editorial inquiries. Include the article link, the passage in question and a source we can check.
